Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Universal AdSense, GDPR & CCPA Compliance

At CalcWorld (accessible via calcworld.net), the privacy and computational confidentiality of our visitors is our paramount operational priority. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines the types of information gathered, how it is utilized, and your statutory rights under global data protection frameworks.

1. Client-Side Computational Isolation

CalcWorld operates on a Zero Server-Side Computational Storage framework. When you input sensitive numbers—such as loan balances, interest rates, mortgage down payments, body weight, caloric intake, or cryptographic seeds—those calculations are performed purely within your device's web browser using native client-side JavaScript.

None of your numerical inputs, personal lifestyle data, or generated financial schedules are ever transmitted to, inspected by, or stored upon our web servers.

2. Web Server Log Files

Like most standard web hosting architectures (such as Cloudflare Pages and standard reverse proxies), CalcWorld utilizes standard web server log files for operational reliability, DDoS mitigation, and traffic diagnostic purposes. The information inside these log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of page views. These logs contain no personally identifiable financial or medical information.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

CalcWorld is financed through non-intrusive digital advertising to keep all 207 calculators 100% free for users globally. Google is one of the third-party vendors operating on our site:

  • Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve targeted advertisements based on a user's prior visits to CalcWorld and other sites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official Ads Settings or via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at aboutads.info.
  • Third-party ad networks or ad servers may use cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons within their respective advertisements appearing on CalcWorld. CalcWorld has no access to or control over these third-party cookies.

4. Local Storage (LocalStorage)

CalcWorld uses browser localStorage exclusively for user convenience features: remembering your visual dark/light theme preference (localStorage.theme) and saving your bookmarked favorite calculators. This data remains on your physical device and is never synchronized to our servers.

5. European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA) possess specific statutory rights regarding their personal data:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The Right to Rectification & Erasure: You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate information or erase your data from server logs.
  • The Right to Restrict & Object to Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data under certain conditions.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers hold the right to request disclosure of categories of personal data collected, request deletion of collected personal data, and demand that a business not sell their personal data. CalcWorld does NOT sell, rent, or trade user personal data to third parties.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

CalcWorld does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that CalcWorld has recorded such information in its log files, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such records.

8. Consent & Policy Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update our Privacy Policy periodically; any modifications will be reflected immediately on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contacting Our Data Protection Officer

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact our administrative team through our Contact & Support Page or via email at privacy@calcworld.net.
